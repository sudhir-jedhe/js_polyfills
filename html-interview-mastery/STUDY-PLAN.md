# Suggested Study Plan

A 2-week plan, ~45–60 min/day.

## Week 1 — Structure, forms & accessibility

- Day 1–2: `01-semantic-html-document-structure`
- Day 3–4: `02-forms-validation`
- Day 5–7: `03-accessibility-aria` (the biggest topic and the one interviewers probe hardest — spend extra time, especially focus management and the "no ARIA is better than bad ARIA" rule)

## Week 2 — Media, APIs, performance & SEO

- Day 1: `04-media-embeds`
- Day 2–3: `05-html5-apis-storage` (storage comparison table, History API)
- Day 4: `06-performance-loading-attributes` (defer vs async execution order — memorize this cold)
- Day 5: `07-metadata-seo-head`
- Day 6–7: Full review — every `interview-qa/` folder, timed self-quiz

## Interview-week fast pass

1. `output-based/` for `06-performance-loading-attributes` (script loading order) and `05-html5-apis-storage`
   (storage comparison) — these come up constantly
2. `scenarios/` for `03-accessibility-aria` (modal focus trap, accessible custom dropdown) — a very common
   live-coding ask
3. Comparison tables inside `theory/` across the repo — localStorage vs sessionStorage vs cookies vs
   IndexedDB, defer vs async, picture vs img, robots meta vs robots.txt
4. `problems/` if you have time
