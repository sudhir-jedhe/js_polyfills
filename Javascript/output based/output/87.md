**Core Rule (`A instanceof B`):**

* `instanceof` tests whether the prototype property of a constructor (`B.prototype`) appears anywhere in the prototype chain of object `A`.

**The Prototype Chains:**

* Both `Function` and `Object` are built-in constructor functions.
* `Function.__proto__ === Function.prototype`
* `Object.__proto__ === Function.prototype`
* `Function.prototype.__proto__ === Object.prototype`
* `Object.prototype.__proto__ === null`
