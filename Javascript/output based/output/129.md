### Step-by-Step Breakdown

**Fundamental Prototype Chain Facts in JavaScript:**

1. Built-in constructors (`Object`, `Function`, `Array`, `Map`) are functions created by `Function`.

* `AnyConstructor.__proto__ === Function.prototype`
* `AnyConstructor.constructor === Function`

1. `Function.prototype` is an object whose prototype inherits from `Object.prototype`:

* `Function.prototype.__proto__ === Object.prototype`
* `Object.prototype.__proto__ === null`

1. `a instanceof B` evaluates whether `B.prototype` exists anywhere along `a`'s `__proto__` chain.

---

1. **`Function.prototype.__proto__ === Object.prototype` $\rightarrow$ `true**`

* The prototype object of functions inherits from `Object.prototype`.

1. **`Function.__proto__ === Object.__proto__` $\rightarrow$ `true**`

* Both `Function` and `Object` are constructor functions, so both have `__proto__ === Function.prototype`.

1. **`Function.__proto__.__proto__ === Object.prototype` $\rightarrow$ `true**`

* `Function.__proto__` is `Function.prototype`.
* `Function.prototype.__proto__` is `Object.prototype`.

1. **`Object.constructor.prototype === Object.prototype` $\rightarrow$ `false**`

* `Object.constructor` is `Function`.
* `Function.prototype !== Object.prototype`.

1. **`Function.constructor === Function` $\rightarrow$ `true**`

* `Function` was constructed by itself (`Function`).

1. **`Object.constructor === Object` $\rightarrow$ `false**`

* `Object` is a constructor function created by `Function`, so `Object.constructor === Function`.

1. **`Array.__proto__ === Function.__proto__` $\rightarrow$ `true**`

* Both are built-in constructor functions and share `Function.prototype` as their `__proto__`.

1. **`Array.constructor === Function` $\rightarrow$ `true**`

* `Array` is a function created by the `Function` constructor.

1. **`Object.__proto__ === Function` $\rightarrow$ `false**`

* `Object.__proto__` is `Function.prototype` (an object/empty function), not the `Function` constructor itself.

 1. **`Function.__proto__ === Function.prototype` $\rightarrow$ `true**`

* In JS, the prototype of the `Function` constructor is `Function.prototype`.

 1. **`Object instanceof Object` $\rightarrow$ `true**`

* Checks if `Object.prototype` is in `Object`'s prototype chain:
`Object` $\rightarrow$ `Function.prototype` $\rightarrow$ `Object.prototype`. (Found).

 1. **`Function instanceof Function` $\rightarrow$ `true**`

* Checks if `Function.prototype` is in `Function`'s prototype chain:
`Function` $\rightarrow$ `Function.prototype`. (Found).

 1. **`Map instanceof Map` $\rightarrow$ `false**`
