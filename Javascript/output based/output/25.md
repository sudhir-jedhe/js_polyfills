### Step-by-Step Breakdown

1. **`1 / 0` $\rightarrow$ `Infinity**`

* Dividing a positive finite number by positive zero results in `Infinity`.

1. **`-1 / 0` $\rightarrow$ `-Infinity**`

* Dividing a negative number by positive zero results in `-Infinity`.

1. **`0 / 0` $\rightarrow$ `NaN**`

* Indeterminate mathematical forms evaluate to `NaN` (Not a Number).

1. **`0 === -0` $\rightarrow$ `true**`

* Strict equality (`===`) treats signed zeros (`+0` and `-0`) as strictly equal.

1. **`Object.is(0, -0)` $\rightarrow$ `false**`

* `Object.is` implements the *SameValue* algorithm and distinguishes between `+0` and `-0`.

1. **`Object.is(0, Math.round(-0.5))` $\rightarrow$ `false**`

* `Math.round(x)` rounds half-integers toward $+\infty$. Rounding `-0.5` toward $+\infty$ yields `-0`.
* `Object.is(0, -0)` evaluates to `false`.

1. **`Object.is(0, Math.round(0.5))` $\rightarrow$ `false**`

* `Math.round(0.5)` rounds up to `1`.
* `Object.is(0, 1)` evaluates to `false`.

1. **`0 * Infinity` $\rightarrow$ `NaN**`

* Multiplying zero by infinity is indeterminate in IEEE 754 floating-point arithmetic.

1. **`Infinity / Infinity` $\rightarrow$ `NaN**`

* Dividing infinity by infinity is an indeterminate form.

 1. **`Object.is(0, Math.sign(0))` $\rightarrow$ `true**`

* `Math.sign(0)` preserves the sign and returns `0` (or `+0`).
* `Object.is(0, 0)` is `true`.

 1. **`Object.is(0, Math.sign(-0))` $\rightarrow$ `false**`

* `Math.sign(-0)` returns `-0`.
* `Object.is(0, -0)` evaluates to `false`.

 1. **`1 / -0` $\rightarrow$ `-Infinity**`

* Dividing a positive number by negative zero results in `-Infinity`.

 1. **`1 / 0` $\rightarrow$ `Infinity**`

* Standard positive division by zero results in `Infinity`.

 1. **`1n / 0n` $\rightarrow$ Throws `RangeError: Division by zero**`

* Unlike IEEE 754 Numbers, `BigInt` operations do not support `Infinity` or `NaN`. Any division or modulo by `0n` throws a runtime `RangeError`.
