# Suggested Study Plan

A 3-week plan, ~45–60 min/day. Do this after `js-interview-mastery` and `react-interview-mastery`.

## Week 1 — App Router fundamentals & rendering

- Day 1–2: `01-app-router-basics`
- Day 3–4: `02-rendering-strategies` (SSR/SSG/ISR/PPR — heavily tested, spend extra time)
- Day 5–6: `03-server-client-components` (the single most-asked Next.js interview topic right now)
- Day 7: Review — `output-based/` for topics 1–3

## Week 2 — Data, routing & server-side plumbing

- Day 1–2: `04-data-fetching-caching`
- Day 3: `05-api-route-handlers` (build/run `projects/mini-notes-api/`)
- Day 4: `06-middleware`
- Day 5: `09-dynamic-routes-params`
- Day 6: `10-navigation-linking`
- Day 7: Review — `scenarios/` for topics 4, 5, 6, 9

## Week 3 — Optimization, SEO, deployment & polish

- Day 1: `07-metadata-seo`
- Day 2: `08-image-font-optimization`
- Day 3–4: `11-deployment-performance`
- Day 5: `12-patterns-anti-patterns`
- Day 6: Read through `job-applications-tracker-nextjs` in `js_polyfills/Next JS/` (see `SOURCE-MAP.md`) — a
  real production-shaped app that ties everything together
- Day 7: Full review — every `interview-qa/` folder, timed self-quiz

## Interview-week fast pass

1. `output-based/` for `02-rendering-strategies`, `03-server-client-components`, `04-data-fetching-caching`
   (this is where most Next.js interviews actually probe)
2. `scenarios/` for `05-api-route-handlers`, `06-middleware`, `09-dynamic-routes-params`
3. Comparison tables inside `theory/` across the whole repo — SSR vs SSG vs ISR, Server vs Client Components,
   `next/link` vs `<a>`, App Router vs Pages Router
4. `problems/` across the repo if you have time
5. Skim the real project (`job-applications-tracker-nextjs`) — being able to talk through a real
   Server-Actions + Drizzle + auth app is worth more than any memorized answer
